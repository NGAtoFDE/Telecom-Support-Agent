# International Roaming — Activation Runbook

## Overview
Use this runbook to help a customer activate international roaming before travelling
abroad, or to fix a data or calling problem while roaming.

## Steps
1. Confirm the destination country and travel dates.
2. Check the account is eligible for roaming (postpaid active, or prepaid with roaming
   enabled) and that KYC is complete.
3. Activate an international roaming pack that covers the destination via the self-care app
   or by dialling the roaming USSD; a pack is cheaper than pay-as-you-go rates.
4. Ask the customer to enable "Data Roaming" in device settings after landing.
5. If no network appears abroad, manually select a partner network under Network Operators.

## Escalation
If roaming is active and paid but no partner network registers after manual selection,
route to ROAMING_PARTNER_DESK with the country and partner network name.
