# Billing Dispute — Investigation Runbook

## Overview
Use this runbook when a postpaid customer disputes a charge on their bill: an unexpected
amount, a charge for a service they did not use, or a plan rental that looks wrong. Money
decisions are never made by the assistant; the outcome is always a routed ticket to
Billing Operations for a human to action.

## Steps
1. Identify the exact disputed line item and the billing cycle it appears on.
2. Check whether the charge is a one-time item (roaming, VAS, add-on) or recurring rental.
3. Confirm whether the customer knowingly activated any pack that maps to the charge.
4. Explain the charge from the itemised bill if it is legitimate and documented.
5. If the charge is unexplained or contested, capture details and route to BILLING_OPS.

## Important
Do not promise a refund, waiver or adjustment. All billing adjustments require a human in
Billing Operations and are governed by the billing adjustment policy.
