# General Escalation and Handover — Runbook

## Overview
Use this runbook for issues that do not fit a specific category, or when a customer request
is out of scope for self-service. The safe default is to capture context and route to the
General L1 human queue.

## Steps
1. Summarise the customer's request in one or two sentences in neutral language.
2. Confirm the customer's circle and account type (prepaid/postpaid) for context.
3. Check whether any specific category runbook applies before defaulting to general.
4. If no specific runbook fits, create a ticket to GENERAL_L1 with the summary.
5. Give the customer the ticket reference and set the expectation of a human follow-up.

## Note
Out-of-scope requests (legal, complex disputes, anything requiring account changes) are
always handed to a human. Do not attempt actions outside approved guidance.
