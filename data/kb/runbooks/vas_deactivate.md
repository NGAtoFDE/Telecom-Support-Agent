# Deactivate an Unwanted VAS Subscription — Runbook

## Overview
Use this runbook when a customer wants to stop a Value Added Service (VAS) such as a caller
tune, a subscription pack, or a content service that is charging them.

## Steps
1. Identify the active VAS from the bill line item or the self-care app under
   "My Subscriptions".
2. Deactivate from the self-care app, or dial `*121*5#` to view and cancel active packs.
3. To stop all commercial promotional messages, activate DND (Do Not Disturb) via SMS.
4. Confirm the deactivation SMS is received and note the effective date.
5. If the customer disputes past charges for a VAS they did not consent to, capture details
   and route to BILLING_OPS for a possible reversal.

## Note
Deactivation stops future charges. Refunds for past charges are a billing decision, not an
assistant decision.
