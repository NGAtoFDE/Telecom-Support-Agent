# New SIM Activation — Runbook

## Overview
Use this runbook for a customer whose new prepaid or postpaid SIM has not activated after
purchase. Activation depends on completed KYC and a tele-verification step.

## Steps
1. Confirm KYC was completed at the point of sale and the correct SIM is inserted.
2. Ask the customer to restart the handset so the new SIM registers on the network.
3. Confirm the activation SMS was received; activation typically completes within 24 hours.
4. Guide the customer through tele-verification by dialling the activation number when
   prompted and answering the identity questions.
5. If more than 24 hours have passed with completed KYC, route to SIM_PROVISIONING.

## Note
A SIM will not activate on incomplete or mismatched KYC. If KYC is the blocker, follow the
KYC re-verification runbook first.
