# KYC Re-verification — Runbook

## Overview
Use this runbook when a connection is flagged for KYC re-verification, or a customer's
number is suspended pending KYC. Completing re-verification restores service.

## Steps
1. Explain why re-verification is required (audit flag, document expiry, or name mismatch).
2. Ask the customer to complete e-KYC via the self-care app or visit a store with a valid
   government ID and address proof.
3. Confirm the name and details on the document match the connection records.
4. Submit the re-verification; a live photo capture may be required.
5. Advise that service is typically restored within 24 hours of successful re-verification.

## Escalation
If service is not restored 24 hours after a successful, matching re-verification, route to
ACCOUNT/GENERAL_L1 with the request reference.
