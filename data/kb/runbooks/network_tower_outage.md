# Suspected Tower / Circle Outage — NOC Handover

## Overview
A regional outage affects many customers in one circle at once. Symptoms: total loss of
voice and data for everyone in an area, or "no service" clustered around specific pin
codes. Do not attempt device-level fixes for a confirmed outage; capture details and hand
over to the NOC.

## Steps
1. Ask for the circle (e.g. Maharashtra, Delhi) and the nearest landmark or pin code.
2. Note the exact time service was lost and whether both voice and data are affected.
3. Check the internal outage board for an existing incident in that circle.
4. If an incident exists, share the reference and the expected restoration window.
5. If none exists, raise a P1 ticket to NOC_L2 with circle, pin code and affected count.

## Customer messaging
Acknowledge the disruption, avoid committing a fixed restoration time unless the NOC has
published one, and offer to send an SMS update when service is restored.
