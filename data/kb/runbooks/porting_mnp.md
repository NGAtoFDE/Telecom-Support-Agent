# Mobile Number Portability (MNP) — Porting Runbook

## Overview
Use this runbook to help a customer port their existing number to our network (port-in) or
understand a delayed port. Porting uses a Unique Porting Code (UPC).

## Steps
1. Ask the customer to SMS `PORT <mobile number>` to 1900 to receive the UPC.
2. Confirm the UPC and its validity date; a UPC is time-limited and must be used before it
   expires.
3. Complete KYC and submit the porting request with the UPC at a store or online.
4. Explain the porting window: intra-circle ports typically complete in about 3 working
   days; inter-circle ports can take longer.
5. Advise that a short service interruption occurs at the moment of switchover, usually
   notified by SMS with the exact date and time.

## Escalation
If a port is stuck beyond the committed window with a valid UPC and completed KYC, route to
SIM_PROVISIONING with the UPC and request date.
