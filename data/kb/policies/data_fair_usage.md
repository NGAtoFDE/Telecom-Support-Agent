# Policy — Fair Usage (FUP) for Data Plans

## Purpose
The Fair Usage Policy ensures consistent service quality for all customers on shared
network capacity. It defines the high-speed data quota after which speeds are reduced.

## How FUP applies
Each unlimited plan lists a daily or monthly high-speed quota. Once consumed, throughput
is reduced to the plan's post-FUP speed (commonly 64 Kbps) until the quota resets. Voice
and SMS are unaffected by data FUP.

## Restoring high-speed data
Customers can purchase a data add-on to restore high-speed access before the reset. FUP
thresholds and post-FUP speeds are published on each plan's page and on the self-care app.
