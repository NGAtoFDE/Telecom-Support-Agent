# Policy — Porting (MNP) Rules and Timelines

## Eligibility
A number can be ported after 90 days on the current operator, with all dues cleared. A
postpaid number must have no outstanding bill. The name on KYC must match the connection.

## Unique Porting Code (UPC)
Porting requires a UPC obtained by SMS `PORT <number>` to 1900. The UPC is valid for a
limited period; if it expires, a fresh code must be generated.

## Timelines and switchover
Intra-circle ports complete in approximately 3 working days; inter-circle and corporate
ports may take longer. A brief outage occurs at switchover, notified in advance by SMS.
Porting cannot be reversed instantly, so confirm the customer's intent before submission.
