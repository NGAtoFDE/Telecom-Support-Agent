# Policy — Network Coverage and Outage SLA

## Coverage commitment
Coverage is provided on a best-effort basis and varies by circle, terrain and indoor
environment. Published coverage maps indicate outdoor coverage and do not guarantee indoor
signal strength at every address.

## Outage restoration targets
For a confirmed circle outage, the target acknowledgement time is 30 minutes and the
target restoration window is communicated by the NOC per incident. Planned maintenance is
notified by SMS at least 24 hours in advance where possible.

## Compensation
Prolonged confirmed outages exceeding 24 continuous hours on a postpaid connection may
qualify for a pro-rata rental waiver on request. Prepaid validity extensions are handled
case by case and require a confirmed NOC incident reference.
