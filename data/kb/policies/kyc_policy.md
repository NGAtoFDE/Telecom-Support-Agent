# Policy — KYC and Connection Verification

## Regulatory basis
Customer identity verification (KYC) is mandatory for every connection under applicable
telecom regulations. The operator must hold valid, matching KYC for each active number.

## Re-verification triggers
Re-verification may be required on audit flags, document expiry, suspected misuse, or a
detected mismatch between the connection and the KYC on record.

## Suspension and restoration
Connections without valid KYC may be suspended after due notice. Service is restored within
24 hours of successful re-verification. No account or billing action is taken as part of
KYC beyond suspension and restoration of service.
