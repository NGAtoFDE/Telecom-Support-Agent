# Policy — Failed Recharge Refund Timeline

## Auto-reconciliation
Payments captured without a completed recharge are auto-reconciled within 30 minutes. The
system either applies the recharge or initiates an automatic refund to the source account.

## Manual refunds
If auto-reconciliation does not resolve the case, a manual refund is initiated by Billing
Operations after verifying the debit against gateway logs using the transaction reference.

## Timelines
- Wallet/UPI refunds: typically 3 to 5 working days.
- Card/net-banking refunds: typically 7 to 10 working days.
The customer is not charged again for a failed recharge, and no successful recharge is
reversed.
