# Policy — Billing Adjustments and Refunds

## Principle
All billing adjustments are made by Billing Operations, never automatically and never by
first-line support. This protects customers and the business and keeps an auditable trail
for every money movement.

## Eligible adjustments
- Charges for a VAS pack activated without valid consent are reversed in full.
- Duplicate charges confirmed against payment logs are refunded.
- Documented, legitimate charges are explained but not reversed.

## Process and timelines
A dispute is logged to BILLING_OPS with the disputed line item, cycle and amount. Standard
resolution is 7 working days. Approved credits appear on the next billing cycle. Refunds to
the original payment method, where applicable, are processed within 7 to 10 working days.
