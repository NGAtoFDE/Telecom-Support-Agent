# Policy — VAS Activation Consent

## Consent requirement
A Value Added Service may only be activated with the customer's explicit, verifiable
consent (double-confirmation). Activation without valid consent is not permitted.

## Reversal of non-consented charges
Where a VAS was activated without valid double-confirmed consent, all associated charges
are reversed in full by Billing Operations. The service is deactivated with immediate
effect.

## Records
Consent records and activation logs are retained so any dispute can be verified. Customers
can view and cancel all active subscriptions from the self-care app at any time.
