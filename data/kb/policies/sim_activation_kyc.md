# Policy — Activation KYC Requirements

## Mandatory KYC
Every new connection requires valid Know Your Customer (KYC) verification before
activation, in line with regulatory requirements. A SIM cannot be activated without it.

## Accepted documents
A government-issued proof of identity and proof of address (for example Aadhaar-based
verification or an accepted alternative) plus a live photo captured at the point of sale.
Documents must match the name on the connection.

## Consequences of failed KYC
Connections found with incomplete or invalid KYC during audit may be suspended until
re-verification is completed. Re-verification follows the KYC re-verification runbook.
