# Policy — Call Drop Handling and Compensation

## Scope
This policy covers repeated call drops attributable to the network rather than the
customer's handset or location. It does not cover drops caused by device faults, flight
mode, or being outside coverage.

## Handling standard
First-line agents must run the call-drop diagnostics runbook and enable VoLTE before
escalating. Persistent network-side drops are logged against the serving cell for the
radio team.

## Compensation
Call-drop compensation is not automatic. Where a confirmed network fault caused sustained
disruption, a goodwill talktime credit may be offered on a postpaid account after review.
Any credit requires a logged fault reference and supervisor approval.
