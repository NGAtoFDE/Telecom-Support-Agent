# Policy — International Roaming Charges

## Packs versus pay-as-you-go
Customers are strongly encouraged to activate an international roaming pack before travel.
Without a pack, usage is billed at pay-as-you-go rates, which are significantly higher and
are a common source of bill shock and disputes.

## What packs cover
Roaming packs bundle a data allowance, outgoing/incoming call minutes and SMS for a set of
countries and a validity period. Incoming calls while abroad may be charged even if not
answered, depending on the pack terms.

## Billing and disputes
Roaming charges appear as separate line items on the next bill. Disputes about roaming
charges follow the standard billing dispute process and route to BILLING_OPS.
