# FAQ — "Money deducted but recharge not done"

## Why did this happen?
The payment was captured by the gateway but the recharge confirmation did not reach your
account in time, often shown as error `ERR-RCH-402`. This is usually a temporary timeout
and reconciles automatically.

## What should I do first?
Wait 30 minutes and check "Recharge history" in the self-care app. Most pending recharges
either complete or auto-refund within this window. Keep the bank/wallet transaction id.

## It has been longer than 30 minutes and I still have no balance.
Raise a ticket with the transaction id. Billing Operations verifies the debit against the
payment logs and either applies the recharge manually or refunds the amount. Refunds to
the source account take 7 to 10 working days.
