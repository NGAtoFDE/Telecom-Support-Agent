# FAQ — Weak Indoor Coverage

## Why is signal weak indoors?
Building materials, basements and metal-framed structures attenuate the mobile signal.
Coverage that is fine outdoors can drop to one or two bars indoors. This is expected
behaviour and is not a SIM or account fault.

## What can I do about it?
Enable Wi-Fi Calling (VoWiFi) so calls route over your home broadband when mobile signal
is weak. Keep the phone near a window on an upper floor for better reception. A signal
booster or femtocell is an option for persistent dead zones in large premises.

## When should this be escalated?
If outdoor coverage at the same address has recently degraded, or neighbours on the same
network are also affected, raise it so the coverage team can check the serving cell site.
