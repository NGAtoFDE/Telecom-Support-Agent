# FAQ — Stores, Documents and Timings

## How do I find the nearest store?
Use the "Store Locator" in the self-care app and allow location access, or search by pin
code. Store timings and the services offered at each store are listed on the store page.

## What should I carry to a store?
For most in-store services carry a valid government photo ID. For a new connection, porting
or KYC re-verification, also carry a proof of address that matches your name.

## Can I do everything online instead?
Many services — recharge, plan change, bill payment, subscription management, e-KYC and
raising a complaint — are available in the self-care app without visiting a store.
