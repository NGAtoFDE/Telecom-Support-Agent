# FAQ — Refund for an Unwanted Subscription

## Can I get a refund for a subscription I never activated?
Yes, if the VAS was activated without your valid consent. First deactivate it to stop
further charges, then raise a dispute. Billing Operations checks the activation and consent
logs and reverses non-consented charges in full.

## How far back can charges be reversed?
Reversals cover charges tied to the non-consented activation as verified against the logs.
Provide the charge dates and amounts from your itemised bill to speed up the review.

## What if I did click "confirm" by mistake once?
Activations captured with valid double-confirmation are treated as consented. The service
is still deactivated on request, but past charges for a consented pack may not be reversed.
