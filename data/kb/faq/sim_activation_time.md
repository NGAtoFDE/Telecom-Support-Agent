# FAQ — How long does SIM activation take?

## Standard activation window
A new SIM usually activates within 24 hours of completed KYC and tele-verification. Many
activate within a few hours. You will receive an SMS when the SIM is live.

## What can delay activation?
Incomplete or mismatched KYC documents are the most common delay. A porting-in request has
its own timeline governed by the porting rules. Weekends and public holidays can add a
short delay to tele-verification.

## My SIM is still not active after 24 hours.
Restart the handset first. If it is still inactive with completed KYC, raise it to SIM
provisioning with the mobile number and the date of purchase.
