# FAQ — KYC and Identity Verification

## Why am I asked to re-verify my KYC?
Re-verification is required by regulation when a connection is flagged in an audit, when a
document expires, or when there is a name or detail mismatch. It protects your number from
misuse.

## What documents do I need?
A valid government-issued proof of identity and proof of address, matching the name on the
connection, plus a live photo where required. Aadhaar-based e-KYC is one accepted method
where offered.

## My number is suspended for KYC — how do I restore it?
Complete e-KYC in the self-care app or at a store. Service is normally restored within 24
hours of a successful, matching re-verification.
