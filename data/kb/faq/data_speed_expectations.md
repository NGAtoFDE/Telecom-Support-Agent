# FAQ — Data Speed Expectations

## Why did my speed drop suddenly mid-month?
Most unlimited plans apply a Fair Usage Policy (FUP). After the high-speed data quota is
used, speeds are reduced (commonly to 64 Kbps) for the rest of the cycle. This is normal
and is restored at the next cycle or with a data add-on.

## How do I check my remaining high-speed data?
Dial `*121#` or check the self-care app under "Usage". The balance shows high-speed data
remaining and the reset date.

## Will a data pack restore full speed?
Yes. A data add-on pack restores high-speed data immediately for the current cycle. The
FUP throttle is lifted as soon as the pack is active.
