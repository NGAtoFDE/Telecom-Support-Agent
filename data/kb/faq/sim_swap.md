# FAQ — SIM Swap / Replacement

## When do I need a SIM swap?
Request a SIM swap if your SIM is lost, stolen, damaged, or you need a different form
factor (for example moving to an eSIM or a nano-SIM). Your mobile number stays the same.

## How does a SIM swap work?
After identity verification, a new SIM is issued against your existing number. The old SIM
stops working once the new one is activated, which protects you if the old SIM was lost.
Activation of the swapped SIM typically completes within a few hours.

## Is SIM swap a security risk?
Unauthorised SIM swap is a known fraud vector, so swaps require strong identity
verification. Never share an OTP with anyone; staff will never ask for your UPI PIN, card
CVV or banking OTP.
