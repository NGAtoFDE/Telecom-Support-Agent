# FAQ — Tethering and Mobile Hotspot

## My internet works but hotspot does not. Why?
Usually the hotspot is off, the second device has the wrong password, or the plan does not
permit tethering. Confirm the APN is `internet`, enable Mobile Hotspot, set a WPA2
password, and reconnect the second device.

## Does hotspot use extra data?
Hotspot data is drawn from your plan's data allowance. On most prepaid plans it counts
against the same quota as on-device data, so heavy tethering can hit the FUP faster.

## Why is my tethered speed slow?
Tethered speed cannot exceed your mobile data speed. If you are past the FUP limit, both
on-device and tethered speeds are throttled until the quota resets or you add a data pack.
