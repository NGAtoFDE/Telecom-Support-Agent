# FAQ — Caller Tune and Subscription Packs

## How do I turn off my caller tune?
Manage or cancel the caller tune from the self-care app under "My Subscriptions", or dial
`*121*5#` to view and deactivate active value-added services. You will get a confirmation
SMS once it is stopped.

## I keep getting charged for a pack I did not ask for.
Deactivate it immediately using the steps above to stop further charges, then raise a
dispute for the charges you did not consent to. Unauthorised VAS charges are reversed under
the billing adjustment policy.

## How do I stop promotional SMS and calls?
Activate DND (Do Not Disturb) from the self-care app or by SMS to block commercial
communication categories you do not want.
