# FAQ — Roaming Before You Travel

## Do I need to do anything before going abroad?
Yes. Activate an international roaming pack for your destination and dates before you fly.
Confirm your account is roaming-eligible and KYC is complete. Packs are much cheaper than
pay-as-you-go rates.

## How do I turn on roaming on my phone abroad?
After landing, enable "Data Roaming" in your device settings and restart the phone so it
searches for a partner network. Set network selection to Auto.

## Will I be charged for incoming calls abroad?
Depending on your pack, incoming calls while roaming may be chargeable even if unanswered.
Check your pack's terms so there are no surprises on the bill.
