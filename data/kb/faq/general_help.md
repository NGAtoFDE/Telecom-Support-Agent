# FAQ — General Help and Self-Care

## How do I check my balance, usage and plan?
Dial `*121#` or open the self-care app. The dashboard shows your balance, data usage, plan
details and the next recharge or bill date.

## How do I reach a human agent?
This assistant handles common issues and routes anything it cannot resolve to the right
specialist team as a ticket. You will receive a reference number when a ticket is raised.

## What can this assistant not do?
It cannot make account-specific commitments, process refunds or billing adjustments, or
take actions on your account. Those are handled by human teams. It only provides guidance
from approved knowledge and raises tickets on your behalf.
