# FAQ — VoLTE and HD Voice

## What is VoLTE and why does it matter for call quality?
VoLTE (Voice over LTE) carries calls over the 4G/5G data network instead of the older 2G/3G
voice network. It gives faster call setup, HD Voice clarity, and lets you use data during a
call. Enabling VoLTE resolves many call-drop and call-setup complaints.

## How do I enable VoLTE?
Go to Settings > Mobile Network and turn on "VoLTE" or "HD Voice". If the toggle is
missing, update your handset software and confirm your plan and SIM are VoLTE-provisioned.

## My phone has no VoLTE option — what now?
Some older handsets do not support VoLTE. Confirm the device is on the supported list; if
not, the customer may experience drops as 2G/3G networks are retired.
