# FAQ — "There is a wrong charge on my bill"

## How do I see what a charge is for?
Open the itemised bill in the self-care app under "Bills". Each charge shows the service,
date and amount. Roaming, VAS subscriptions and one-time add-ons appear as separate line
items from your monthly rental.

## I was charged for a pack I never activated. What happens?
Raise a dispute. The charge is investigated by Billing Operations against activation logs.
If the pack was activated without valid consent, it is reversed per the billing adjustment
policy. Keep the charge date and amount handy.

## How long does a billing dispute take?
Disputes are acknowledged immediately and typically resolved within 7 working days. Any
approved adjustment appears as a credit on the next bill.
